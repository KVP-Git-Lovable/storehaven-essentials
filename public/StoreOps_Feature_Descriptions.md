# StoreOps Feature Descriptions

## Asset Ops

### Store Asset
Centralized Asset Register tracking every physical asset across all stores—including purchase details, condition, warranty, deployment location, and full lifecycle status history.

### Service Contract
Manages AMC, Warranty, and On-Call agreements with vendors. Includes SLA matrices (P1–P4), escalation levels, covered assets/locations, and auto-generates recurring PM tasks for the contract duration.

### Preventive Maintenance Plans and Schedules
Calendar-driven scheduling of recurring maintenance tasks (Inspections, Deep Cleaning, Calibration) linked to specific assets and stores, with day/week/month views and overdue tracking.

### Ability to Record a Ticket or an Issue
Service Tickets module for logging corrective maintenance requests against specific assets and stores, with category tagging, SLA tracking, and auto-linked service contracts.

### Cost of Service (Components, Labor, Vendor Bills)
Each service ticket and PM task captures spares consumed, labour hours, and vendor charges—providing full cost visibility per incident and asset.

### Knowledge Base
A searchable library of troubleshooting guides, SOPs, and procedures linked to asset types and categories. Supports AI-assisted article generation from uploaded documents or videos.

### Visual Merchandise and AI-Driven Audits
Stores submit VM photos against planogram standards. An AI compliance engine analyzes submissions, scores adherence, and auto-generates corrective tasks for non-compliant displays.

### Store Admin Task Automation
Role-based daily task engine where checklists are auto-assigned by shift and operating hours. Tracks completion, supports shift handovers, and feeds into a store-wide Task Adherence heatmap.

### Spare Inventory (Requisition, GRN, Visibility)
Full inventory lifecycle—from raising purchase requisitions and recording Goods Receipt Notes (with quality checks) to tracking stock levels, consumption logs, expiry management, and low-stock alerts per store.

## Store Financials

### Store Rentals
Tracks rental agreements per store including lease terms, rent amounts, escalation schedules, and payment history.

### Store Budget Management
Annual and monthly budgeting by store type using standardized templates (Groups & Items). Supports a formal approval workflow (Draft → Approved) with budgeted vs. actual variance tracking and store-wise adherence leaderboards.

### Store Petty Cash
Per-store petty cash allocation with expense logging. Automatically rolls up totals to show available vs. spent balances for each store location.

### Daily Usage Meters Reading
Utility meter readings (Electricity, Water, Solar, etc.) driven by a Meter Master that defines parameters and capture methods. Auto-calculates derived values like Consumed Units and Power Factor.

### Store Target vs. Actuals
Tracks store-level performance targets against actual results, enabling management to monitor revenue and operational KPI adherence across the retail network.

## NSO (New Store Onboarding)

### New Store Task Master (by Type, Size, or Location)
Reusable NSO Checklist Master templates that define standardized tasks with durations, buildup offsets, vendors, and attachments—customizable by store type and size.

### New Store Task List with Status and Gantt Charts
Store-specific task instances auto-generated from templates, with absolute dates, status tracking (Open/Completed/Cancelled/Overdue), and a Gantt chart for visual timeline management.

### New Store Asset Deployment Tracker
Tracks which assets from the Asset Register need to be procured and deployed to the new store, linked directly to NSO project tasks.

### New Store Budget and Actuals
Template-level budget line items (with category, vendor, planned amount) are cloned to each store project. Prescribed budgets are auto-calculated using a Sq Ft Budget Master rate × store size.

### New Store Progress Tracker
A project-level dashboard showing overall completion percentage, start/end dates, overdue task counts, and key milestone status for each NSO project.

### New Store Risk Profile (AI Automation)
AI-powered risk assessment that analyzes NSO project data—task delays, budget overruns, and resource gaps—to flag potential launch risks and recommend corrective actions.

## Business Development and Store Identification

### Store Coverage Growth Plan
The Store Plans (Expansion) module manages the retail network growth strategy, tracking target locations, identification dates, status, and expansion budgets.

### Activities to Meet the Growth Plan
Action items and milestones linked to each expansion plan, tracking the operational steps needed to achieve network growth targets.

### Site Pipeline and Approval (Evaluation Checklist)
Potential store locations go through a structured evaluation with photo/video site assessments and a multi-level sequential approval workflow before finalization.

### Franchisee Pipeline and Approval
End-to-end franchisee lifecycle management—from prospecting and application review through onboarding status tracking, with automated identification flags when a franchisee becomes active.

### Site Finalisation and Onboarding Plan
Once a site is approved, it transitions into an onboarding plan that links directly to NSO checklists, budgets, and asset deployment for execution.

### Franchisee Onboarding Finalisation Process
Manages the final steps of bringing a franchisee on board—including agreement signing, training, store setup coordination, and handover to the operations team.
